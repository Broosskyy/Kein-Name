## Representative upgrade effects

- Attack: damage, projectile scale/count, rate and mutation hooks.
- Defense: max HP and mitigation.
- Movement: speed.
- Utility: pickup radius and XP gain.
- Visual hooks: large/split shots, orbitals, shield/magnet, crystal growth, Void orbit, wing feathers and ember vines.

Nothing in the rightmost column is simulated, faked or presented as live in M06.
